01 · Overview

Learning harmony through interaction

Diatomic Harmony connects music learning with interface design. The project explores a tool for working with music theory and chord progressions while learning keyboard and developing songwriting practice.

This first portfolio draft centers the motivation and design opportunity. Implementation details will be added only after the original project materials are gathered and verified.

02 · Motivation and problem

Creative education is not equally accessible

I wanted to learn music theory, chord progressions, and songwriting while learning keyboard, but private music instruction can be expensive or inaccessible. I created Diatomic Harmony to explore how technology could support independent music learning and practice.

03 · Research or design question

How might an interface teach through exploration?

How might an interactive tool make relationships between chords easier to explore for an independent learner developing a songwriting practice?

08 · Challenges and reflections

Designing between theory and play

The core design tension is how to make a formal system approachable without reducing music-making to rules. The case study can become stronger by showing which concepts were difficult to represent and how interaction supported—or complicated—learning.
