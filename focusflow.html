01 · Overview

Make room for thoughts before organizing them

FocusFlow is an existing prototype centered on a Brain Dump interface. It explores how a product might accept unstructured thoughts first, then support organization with clarity and cognitive accessibility in mind.

02 · Motivation and problem

Structure can help—but only at the right moment

Many productivity experiences ask people to categorize a thought before they have fully expressed it. FocusFlow begins with lower-friction capture, then considers how the interface can help create structure without adding cognitive load.

03 · Research or design question

From mental clutter to useful structure

How might a Brain Dump interface help people organize unstructured thoughts while remaining clear, usable, and cognitively accessible?
