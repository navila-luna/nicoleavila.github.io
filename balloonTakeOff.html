Balloon Take Off

A visual and auditory breathing experience shaped by a personal memory of learning how imagination and deep breathing could create space during anxiety.

1 · Overview

Breathing through a visual story

Balloon Take Off is an interactive experience for practicing box breathing and the 4-7-8 breathing technique. It combines writing, guided breathing, animation, and sound to move through a moment of worry and toward release.

2 · Origin

A memory of learning to pause

The project was inspired by the breathing technique my sixth-grade English teacher taught me when I experienced my first panic attack in middle school. My teacher took me outside, sat with me, and asked me to imagine holding a deflated balloon.

I imagined placing everything I was worried about inside it, inflating it through deep breaths, and then lifting it into the air and watching it travel into the sky. Balloon Take Off recreates the way I pictured and felt that moment.

3 · Experience flow

Write, breathe, release

Write

Put worries into words before beginning the breathing exercise.

Breathe

Complete three repetitions of guided deep breathing.

Release

Watch the balloon rise and carry the written worries into the sky.

4 · Implementation and sound

Building atmosphere through code and music

I used Three.js to create the animated balloon and star-filled 3D environment, with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript supporting the interface and experience flow.

I created the music using Max, Reaper, and sampled sounds. When I write, sing, play, or produce music, I often picture a scene from a film or music video. The sound for Balloon Take Off extends that way of working into an interactive story of serenity.
