Balloon Take Off

A visual and auditory breathing experience shaped by a personal memory of learning how imagination and deep breathing could create space during anxiety.

1 · Overview & Motivation

Balloon Take Off is my undergraduate thesis project, an interactive audiovisual experience combining guided breathing, writing, animation, and original music.

The project was inspired by my first panic attack in middle school, when my sixth-grade English teacher, Mr. Jester, introduced me to the 4-7-8 breathing technique. He took me outside, sat with me, and asked me to imagine holding a deflated balloon. As I took deep breaths, I pictured placing my worries inside the balloon, inflating it, and eventually releasing it into the sky.

Years later, I wanted to recreate not just the breathing exercise, but how I imagined and experienced that moment. Through visuals, music, guided breathing, and written reflection, Balloon Take Off invites one to engage multiple senses as they imagine releasing their own worries.

This was my first experience combining computer science, electronic music, and a deeply personal memory into an expressive work. It was also the first time I incorporated my own voice into an interactive piece. Sharing it with my classmates challenged me to be vulnerable through my art and showed me how technology and music could help me communicate something meaningful to others.

2 · Experience flow

Write

Put worries into words before beginning the breathing exercise.

Breathe

Complete three repetitions of guided deep breathing.

Release

Watch the balloon rise and carry the written worries into the sky.

3 · Implementation and sound

I used Three.js to create the animated balloon and star-filled 3D environment, with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript supporting the interface and experience flow.

I created the music using Max, Reaper, and sampled sounds. When I write, sing, play, or produce music, I often picture a scene from a film or music video. The sound for Balloon Take Off extends that way of working into an interactive story of serenity.

4 · Lessons Learned

This project strengthened my skills in creative coding, interactive design, and electronic music production as I learned to combine these disciplines into a cohesive experience. It also challenged me to approach art more personally, using my own voice and memories to create something that others could engage with.
