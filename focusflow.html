FocusFlow

An ADHD-focused work-planning tool for turning goals and unstructured thoughts into prioritized, approachable next steps.

1 · Overview

FocusFlow explores how to help people with ADHD organize actionable work and begin without becoming overwhelmed by the planning process. Its main planning page already lets users define goals, break them into subtasks, rank priorities, use a timer, and ask AI for help creating an approachable plan.

The Brain Dump is an extension currently in progress. Its planned flow will accept typed or transcribed speech, use AI to group and analyze the user’s thoughts, and turn them into actionable items for the main planning view.

The concept is related to MoodBirdie, but its scope is narrower and more task-specific. By designing for a group that can experience challenges with executive functions such as initiation, planning, and organization—and by drawing on my own experience with ADHD—I can explore patterns that may also inform the broader MoodBirdie concept.

2 · Motivation and problem

A work task can feel difficult to begin when it contains too many decisions: what matters first, how the work should be divided, and which step is small enough to start now. FocusFlow concentrates on actionable work rather than well-being, using structure and AI-assisted planning to reduce the effort required to move from an objective to a first step.

3 · Product design question

How might a work-planning tool help people with ADHD turn goals and unstructured thoughts into prioritized, manageable steps they can begin?

4 · Product flow

Capture

Start with a defined work goal; the in-progress Brain Dump will add typed and spoken capture for less structured thoughts.

Structure

Organize the objective into goals and subtasks, with working AI assistance for writing a simpler starting plan.

Prioritize

Rank goals and subtasks so the next action is easier to identify.

Focus

Use the timer and task list together to work through one manageable step at a time.

5 · Product components

  • Working: Goals with nested subtasks, priority ranking, a focus timer, and AI-assisted plan writing on the main planning page.
  • Current AI: The planning feature uses the Gemini API with an earlier model. This phase prioritizes getting the product behavior and feature flow right before investing further in model selection, prompting, or tuning.
  • In progress: A redesigned Brain Dump interface, AI grouping that converts unstructured input into actionable items, and speech transcription for spoken capture.

Design and development

I created interface mockups in Figma before implementing FocusFlow with React, HTML, CSS, Zustand for state management, and Tone.js for timer sounds. I also used Claude as a development support tool.
