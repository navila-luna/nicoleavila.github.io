1 · Overview & Motivation

FocusFlow began with a “what if?” question inspired by my experience with ADHD: What if I combined the Pomodoro technique with an interactive workspace where users could organize tasks and work through them in real time?

I wanted to explore how technology could make planning and task initiation more approachable. Drawing from Agile sprint planning practices I learned through software engineering and strategies I learned in therapy for managing ADHD, I designed a workspace where users can organize goals, break them into subtasks, set priorities, and track progress alongside a focus timer.

One of my main design interests was reducing the friction of getting started. I incorporated AI-assisted planning using microchunking, breaking overwhelming tasks into small, manageable actions. For example, rather than suggesting someone “write a report,” the first step might simply be opening a blank document.

Developing FocusFlow has allowed me to combine my interests in product design, wellbeing, and AI assistance while exploring how technology might help people feel more capable of managing their goals independently. The project remains an evolving prototype, with future work focused on making it easier to capture unstructured thoughts and turn them into actionable plans.

2 · Product flow

Capture

The in-progress Brain Dump is an optional way to capture typed or spoken thoughts, whether or not the user has already defined a goal.

Structure

Organize an objective into goals and subtasks, with optional AI assistance for creating a simpler starting plan.

Prioritize

Rank goals and subtasks so the next action is easier to identify.

Focus

Use the timer and task list together to work through one manageable step at a time.

3 · Implementation

FocusFlow is a web application I independently designed and developed using:

  • Figma: Designed and prototyped the user interface and task-management workflows.
  • React & TypeScript: Built the interactive workspace, task-management components, and focus timer.
  • Zustand: Managed application state, including goals, tasks, priorities, and progress.
  • Google Gemini API: Integrated AI-assisted task planning and microchunking to break larger goals into manageable steps.

The project gave me an opportunity to translate my Figma designs into a functional application while exploring how AI assistance could fit into an interactive productivity tool.

4 · Lessons Learned

This project strengthened my ability to translate Figma designs into functional frontend features while applying best practices in component design and state management. It also expanded my experience with React through building an interactive task-management application.
